# Debate report template

```markdown
## Debate: [question as debated]

Format: [honest positions | assigned sides]. Rounds: opening, [N] rebuttals,
closing. Ended because: [all participants said nothing further | no new
arguments in the last round | converged | fixed round count | user stopped it]. [If any model was unavailable: "Requested [MODEL] but it was not
available in this harness; the debate ran with [list]."]

| Participant | Final position | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Model A] | [one line] | 8/10 |
| [Model B] | [one line] | 6/10 |
| [Model C] | [one line] | no score |

### Points of agreement

- [Point]. [Which participants conceded it during the debate, if any.]

### Points of disagreement

- **[Point].** [Model A] argues [strongest argument]. [Model B] argues
  [strongest counter].

### Arguments that didn't survive

- [Claim] ([who made it]) was rebutted by [who] on the grounds that [why], and
  was not defended again.

### The crux

[One or two sentences on what the disagreement actually turns on. Often a
factual question that could be checked, or a values question the user has to
decide.]

### Moderator's assessment

[Which case was stronger and why, in two to four sentences. Say clearly where
this is your judgement rather than something a participant argued.]

### Recommended next steps

- [Step, only if the question was a decision the user has to make.]

<details>
<summary>Full transcript</summary>

#### Opening statements
...
#### Rebuttal round 1
...
#### Closing statements
...

</details>
```

If a participant gave no score, write `no score`. Do not invent one.
